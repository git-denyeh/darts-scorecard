Tellico Village Dart League Rules

As of 9/6/2026 · Source: Dart League Rules 2026.pdf (Drive modified 2026-09-17)

Goals

  1. Meet new people
  2. Provide the opportunity to learn the game of darts and improve
  3. Have FUN!

Mission Statement

The league seeks to promote the playing and enjoyment of darts in a social environment. All players should realize we are guests of the Yacht Club and the Kahite Pub: we need to start and finish games promptly in order to vacate the premises on time. In addition, the players are responsible for setting up the boards, taking down and storing the equipment and cleaning up their space after their match.

Rules

  • The Tellico Village Dart League has zero tolerance concerning physical and/or verbal abuse. Any violation will result in immediate ejection from the league.
  • Excessive swearing or badgering of players will not be tolerated. Violators may be subject to disciplinary action.
  • Only Tellico Village residents are permitted to play in the league. Non-residents currently in the league are grandfathered into the league.
  • Play will be at the Yacht Club on Tuesday from 6 – 9 pm and at the Kahite Pub on Thursday from 2 – 5 pm unless otherwise noted.
  • Any games not started by 9 pm or 5 pm, respectively will be considered a loss for both teams.
  • To compete in the league a team must have 4 or more players.
  • If a team has fewer than 4 players for a match, they will forfeit the 4 person game.
  • Each team should designate a captain. Captains will be responsible for:
    • Ensuring their team behaves in a sportsmanlike manner.
    • Obtain subs as needed.
    • Resolve minor disputes during a match with the opposing captain. If the team captains cannot come to a final resolution, the matter should be brought to either Kevin Lionarons or Ray Hoppenjans.
    • Team communications throughout the season.
    • Creating the roster for a match.
    • Keeping track of score sheet data.
  • A “match” will consist of 8 games made up of ‘01 and cricket.
  • One sub list will be maintained for the Tellico Village Dart League. Anyone on the sub list can participate in either location. Only subs currently on the sub list can participate. To get on the list, an email must be sent to Kevin Lionarons (seaswallow@yahoo.com) or Ray Hoppenjans (ray.hoppenjans@gmail.com) containing the person’s name, phone number and email address. No one will be added to the list until all three pieces of information are provided.
  • To be eligible to play in a match, a sub must be added to the sub list by midnight of the previous Sunday. Subs cannot be added on the day of a match.
  • Use of a sub in a game that is not on the sub list will result in the loss of that game.
  • Anyone currently on a team roster can act as a sub at the other location, even if they are not listed on the sub list. Roster players may not sub at their own location.
  • Each player should play a minimum of 3 games in a match. A player has “right of refusal”, meaning he/she can opt out of their games, if desired.

Forfeit Rules

  • If a team cannot play, the captain must notify the other captain and Kevin Lionarons or Ray Hoppenjans as soon as possible. Failure to notify will result in a forfeiture.
  • The notifying captain is responsible for rescheduling the match. The match must be completed as soon as is practical, but must be completed before the final match of the season or the team will forfeit.
  • Both captains must do their best to find a time to play the match. Someone always gets hurt when a forfeit occurs; please try to play the match.
  • In the event of a forfeit, the forfeiting team will receive 2 wins and 6 losses; the opposing team will receive 6 wins and 2 losses.

Diddle / Starting

  • Each game starts with one player from each team throwing at the bullseye (the diddle).
  • Closest to the bull goes first.
  • If the first player throws a bull or double bull, the second player has the option of removing the dart prior to his/her throw or leaving it in the board.
  • If both players hit a single bull, double bull or if both throws are approximately the same distance from the bullseye, the players must re-throw in reverse order.

During Play

  • During play, players should leave the darts in the board until the score has been recognized by the scorekeeper and agreed to by the player.
  • Players may not touch any dart in the board during a turn. If a player touches his/her dart in the board during a turn before all three darts are thrown, the player’s turn will be considered complete. Subsequent darts thrown in that turn will not count.
  • Darts must stick in the board to be counted. If a dart falls out of the board prior to the scorekeeper recognizing the total score for the turn, it will not count.

Great Shots

The Score Sheet includes a section to record “Great Shots.” Please clearly write the person’s name and describe the shot. Great shots include:

  • 5 or more marks in Cricket
  • Throwing three bulls in a single turn in Cricket
  • 100 or more points in ’01
  • High out shots in ’01 (ie. 50 or more)
  • A person’s first time hitting the double for an out shot in ‘01

Long Games

  • If a game is running too long, the captains can decide to end the game.
  • The teams can split the point.
  • Another option for 301/501 is to allow play to continue until the current round is completed. If there is no winner, play continues with a “single” out, meaning the game will end when a team reaches zero (hitting a double is not required). All three darts can be used in a turn.
